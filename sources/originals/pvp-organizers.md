## PVP Brief #1 — ALL IN 2026
**Target:** Directeur(trice) des partenariats / commandites, Mila ou Scale AI
**Language:** Français (Montréal)
**Context:** Warm renewal — BETA Labs already ran ALL IN 2025 (Nexus was a client, confirmed meetings delivered)

### The "Oh Sh*t Moment"
Les commanditaires d'ALL IN 2025 qui ne pouvaient pas mesurer leur pipeline après l'événement ne renouvèleront pas en 2026. Chaque commanditaire perdu = 10 000–50 000 $ de revenus en moins pour Mila/Scale AI. La conversation de renouvellement est active maintenant — et si BETA Labs n'est pas intégré dans les packages 2026 avant qu'ils soient finalisés, l'opportunité est perdue pour un an.

**Data sources used:**
- ALL IN 2025 partnership (Nexus client — confirmed meetings delivered pre-event)
- ALL IN 2026: 16–17 sept., Palais des Congrès de Montréal, 7 500 attendees attendus
- Model: ~15% revenue share to organizer, zero budget required

## PVP Brief #2 — RevStar Summit 2026
**Target:** General Partner ou Chief of Staff, Vasco Ventures ou Boreal Ventures
**Language:** English (Toronto)
**Context:** Cold outreach — no existing relationship. VCs organizing a pure B2B SaaS summit, June 3, 2026.

### The "Oh Sh*t Moment"
The B2B SaaS companies sponsoring RevStar will spend $5K–$15K to exhibit, show up to the floor, collect business cards, and leave without a single attributed pipeline deal. They don't re-sign for 2027. Vasco and Boreal lose sponsor revenue — and their portfolio companies leave empty-handed from an event they organized. The window to build a pre-meeting program into the RevStar package closes in ~3 weeks (78 days to June 3 is already the activation edge).

**Data sources used:**
- RevStar Summit 2026: June 3, Toronto, 1,000 attendees, $395–$585 tickets, organized by Vasco + Boreal Ventures
- BETA Labs existing partnerships: ALL IN (Montreal) + Social Next Ottawa + Toronto
- Standard B2B event economics: average lead cost $200–$400 with no meeting guarantee; $100/confirmed meeting

### Candidate 3 — Market Shift / Competitive Positioning
**7-criteria score: 7/7**

**Message:**

> **Preview:**
> "Other Canadian B2B conferences are adding guaranteed pre-event meetings to their sponsor packages this year."
>
> **Engage:**
> Social Next and ALL IN are now offering confirmed 1:1 meetings as part of their top sponsor tier — booked before the event opens, against each sponsor's exact ICP. Sponsors who've used it at one event now ask for it everywhere they exhibit. With 78 days to RevStar, there's still time to build this into your 2026 packages and position RevStar as the conference where sponsors actually generate pipeline.
>
> **Ask:**
> Would it make sense to connect before you lock in the final sponsor tiers?

---

**Winner: Candidate 2**
The portfolio angle is uniquely suited to a VC organizer — it connects RevStar's sponsor revenue directly to the VCs' own portfolio ROI in a way no other event organizer hears. "Your portfolio companies are the sponsors" transforms a generic pitch into a personalized one. The preview question ("Do they have 15 pre-qualified meetings on the calendar?") creates immediate cognitive dissonance — the answer is obviously "no," and they know it.

Candidate 3 is nearly as strong and works as a follow-up if Candidate 2 goes unanswered — the competitive pressure angle (Social Next + ALL IN already doing this) adds external validation.

**A/B test suggestion:** Test Candidate 2 (portfolio angle) vs. Candidate 3 (competitive/market shift) as a follow-up sequence — send Candidate 2 first, Candidate 3 as Day 5 bump if no reply.

**Subject lines (3):**
1. `Before you finalize the RevStar sponsor packages`
