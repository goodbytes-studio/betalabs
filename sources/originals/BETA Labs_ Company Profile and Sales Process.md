# BETA Labs: Company Profile and Sales Process

## 1. Company Profile

BETA Labs is a Montreal-based startup specializing in AI-driven networking and meeting planning for B2B events. Supported by the Centech accelerator, the company is in its early stages (pre-revenue or very early revenue) and is led by founder Elizabeth Tremblay.

### 1.1. Core Mission and Vision

BETA Labs aims to transform event sponsorship into a predictable, high-ROI revenue channel by selling pre-qualified meetings with High-Intent Ideal Customer Profiles (ICPs) rather than just software.

### 1.2. Go-To-Market (GTM) Strategy

- **Channel Sales Model:** The company partners directly with event organizers, acting as a "value-added service layer" integrated into the organizer’s existing offering.
- **Target Partners:** B2B conference organizers and partnership managers for major events such as Startupfest, ALL IN, and C2 Montréal.
- **Revenue Model:** A performance-based pricing structure of $100 per qualified lead/confirmed meeting, with revenue shared between BETA Labs and the event organizer.

### 1.3. Product Offering: "Premium ICP Connection"

The service functions as an AI-powered "concierge" for event sponsors:

- **Matchmaking:** Uses AI to analyze participant lists and identify high-intent ICPs.
- **Execution:** The team contacts, qualifies, and schedules confirmed meetings for sponsors before the event begins.
- **Integration:** Requires no technical integration from the organizer; only a participant list under a Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA) is needed.

### 1.4. Key Market Differentiators

- **Focus on ROI:** BETA Labs focuses on pipeline generation and qualified meetings, unlike traditional event apps that prioritize "scans" and "visibility."
- **Sponsor Retention:** By guaranteeing a tangible ROI, they help organizers increase sponsor renewal rates.

### 1.5. Current Status and Activity (as of March 2026)

- **Development Stage:** Seed stage with a small team (likely under 10 employees).
- **Website:** Currently hosted on Wix.
- **Public Presence:** Elizabeth Tremblay has been active as a speaker at Fuckup Nights Montréal and participated in E-AI 2026.
- **Regulatory Focus:** The company emphasizes compliance with Quebec’s Law 25 regarding data privacy.

## 2. Sales Process Pipelines

This section outlines the different sales process pipelines observed, from a hypothesized sales motion to real-world examples with specific clients.

### 2.1. Hypothesis Sales Motion

This is the idealized sales process that BETA Labs aims to follow.

| Stage | Step | Description |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Inbound Process** | Email from Event | Initial contact is made after an event. |
| | Strategic Outbound | Proactive outreach to potential clients. |
| | Intent Signal | Identifying signs of interest from the prospect. |
| | Website Visit | The prospect visits the BETA Labs website. |
| | Book a Meeting | The prospect schedules a meeting. |
| **Follow-up Process** | Intro Call | A 30-minute introductory call. |
| | Post-Meeting Email | Sending additional information after the call. |
| **Selection Process** | Proposal Review | The prospect reviews the proposal. |
| | Final Decision | The prospect makes a final decision. |
| **Onboarding Process** | Contract + Invoice Review | Review of the contract and invoice. |
| | Signature + Deposit | The contract is signed and a deposit is made. |
| | ICP Alignment | A 20-minute call to align on the Ideal Customer Profile. |
| | Deliverable | The final service is delivered. |
| | Final Payment | The final payment is made. |
