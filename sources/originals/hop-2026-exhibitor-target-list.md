## The finding that matters most

**HOP's exhibitor base is structurally better suited to BETA Labs than the tech-conference exhibitor base it has been selling to.**

The May 2026 post-mortem identified low ACV as the thing that kills the model — Cyberimpact needing five closes to break even, Axeptio wanting more for $1,500. That problem largely **disappears here.** A majority of HOP's exhibitors sell big-ticket operational things to retailers:

- **Mobico** sells store fit-outs — a single boutique buildout runs tens to hundreds of thousands
- **Boa Concept** sells warehouse conveyor automation — projects in the hundreds of thousands to millions
- **LIDD** sells distribution network design · **Logistique 3DM** sells 3PL contracts · **Groupe Morneau** sells freight
- **Tink**, **Ascendis**, **Mirego**, **Thirdbridge** sell six-figure digital projects

For all of these, **one closed deal repays the $1,500 package 30× to 300×.** That is the cleanest version of the BETA Labs argument, and it is available to roughly two thirds of this list.

**Second finding:** ~9 of the 34 published organizations are institutional and cannot buy — government, sector bodies, non-profits. The real commercial market is **~22 companies**, and the list is still filling toward 50.

## Two openings the list reveals

**1. Lightspeed was HOP 2025's Presenting Partner and is absent from the 2026 list.**

The top-tier sponsor from last year has not renewed. Either the deal is still in progress or it lapsed — and a lapsed presenting partner is *precisely* the sponsor-retention problem BETA Labs' organizer pitch is built on. Elizabeth is 1st degree with **Philippe Ahmaranian, Senior Director Retail Sales North America at Lightspeed**.

Two uses, and the order matters: raise it with CQCD first as evidence of the problem being real at their own event, and only then — with CQCD's blessing — consider the direct contact. Going around the organizer to their lapsed top sponsor without asking would damage the partnership that was just signed.

**2. HOP 2026 has a new "Zone IA" — an AI Zone devoted to AI adoption in retail.**

CQCD is actively recruiting AI vendors into a brand-new zone. Every AI company in Elizabeth's network that sells to retail is a lead for that zone — **Moov AI, Vooban, Glowtify, Intelligence Node, Klever, Fidget/Leav** — which feeds directly into the exhibitor-lead play in the companion file. A brand-new zone is the easiest possible thing to help an organizer fill, and it is a ready-made content hook.
