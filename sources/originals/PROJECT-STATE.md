## What BETA Labs Does

The pre-event targeting layer for B2B events. Works from the organizer's real attendee list under agreement, scores every record against the sponsor's ICP on four dimensions (firmographics, role, tech stack, buying signal), and delivers a shortlist a week out — each match carrying its qualifying signal and a curated opening draft. **$100 per match delivered.** 🔴 BETA Labs does NOT book meetings; the sponsor sends the draft and keeps the relationship. See `outputs/positioning.md`.

Two motions: (1) Acquire event organizer partners → build the catalogue; (2) Acquire sponsors → fill the catalogue.

---

## ICP Sweet Spot

**Motion 2 (Sponsor acquisition):** B2B SaaS or Marketing/Tech services, 20–200 employees, confirmed sponsor/exhibitor at a specific upcoming B2B conference in Quebec or Ontario within 2–8 weeks. Buyer is GM, VP Marketing, CEO, or BDM.

**Key qualifier:** a specific upcoming event creates the urgency that makes this sell itself. No event = 3–5x longer cycle.
