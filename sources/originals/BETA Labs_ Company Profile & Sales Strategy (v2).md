# BETA Labs: Company Profile & Sales Strategy (v2)

This document outlines the company profile, go-to-market strategy, and detailed sales processes for BETA Labs, incorporating key insights from the March 2026 strategy session between Elizabeth Tremblay and Bruno Marchand.

## 1. Company Profile

BETA Labs is a Montreal-based, seed-stage startup specializing in AI-driven networking and meeting planning for B2B events. Supported by the Centech accelerator, the company is led by founder Elizabeth Tremblay.

- **Core Mission:** To transform event sponsorship from a speculative marketing expense into a predictable, high-ROI revenue channel.
- **Public Presence:** Elizabeth Tremblay has been active as a speaker at events like Fuckup Nights Montréal and E-AI 2026.
- **Regulatory Focus:** The company emphasizes strict compliance with Quebec’s Law 25 regarding data privacy.

## 2. Core Strategy & KPIs

### 2.1. The "Event Catalogue" Growth Pillar

A core strategic pillar is the proactive building of an **"Event Catalogue."** Instead of reactively waiting for introductions, BETA Labs must first secure partnerships with a portfolio of high-quality events. These events act as the "building shell" or distribution channel, which can then be filled with sponsor "tenants."

- **Strategic Goal:** Build a robust and diverse catalogue of event partners to create inventory for sponsor sales.
- **Tactical Action:** A planned website feature includes a public-facing page listing all partner events, serving as a lead magnet for inbound sponsors.

### 2.2. Key Performance Indicator: Time to Value

Deal velocity is a critical metric for the sponsor acquisition sales motion.

| Performance Tier | Time to Value (First Contact to Deposit) |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Exceptional (Anomaly)** | 1–2 business days |
| **Ideal Benchmark** | 5–7 business days |
| **Sub-Optimal** | 10+ business days |

## 3. Go-To-Market: A Dual-Sided Sales Model

BETA Labs operates a two-sided sales model, requiring two distinct sales motions targeting different customer profiles.

| Customer Type | Role | Value Proposition |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Primary Customer (Event Organizer)** | Essential Partner / Distribution Channel | "We unlock the hidden value in your attendee list, helping you retain and upsell sponsors by guaranteeing their ROI." |
| **Beneficiary Customer (Sponsor)** | End-User / Paying Client | "We deliver pre-qualified, high-intent meetings, saving you time and ensuring you generate qualified pipeline from your event investment." |

### 3.1. Product Offering: "Premium ICP Connection"

The service acts as an AI-powered concierge for sponsors, with a key differentiator being its data enrichment capabilities.

- **Process:** BETA Labs takes an event's raw attendee list, enriches it with external data sources, and uses this enhanced dataset to identify and schedule meetings with high-intent individuals who match the sponsor's Ideal Customer Profile (ICP).
- **Competitive Edge vs. Event Apps (e.g., Swapcard):** While platforms like Swapcard perform basic matchmaking based on simple industry correlations (described as "Excel formulas"), BETA Labs provides a deeper level of qualification based on true purchase intent and detailed ICP alignment. The core message is: *"A static attendee list is only as valuable as your ability to activate it. We provide the activation layer."*

## 4. Sales Process Pipelines

### 4.1. Sales Motion 1: Acquiring Event Partners (Primary Customer)

This motion is focused on building the "Event Catalogue." The process is currently being formalized, with plans to create a **"Partnership Guide"** to enable event organizers to act as effective resellers (referred to as "Canal 3").

**Hypothesized Event Partner Sales Motion:**

| Stage | Step | Description |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Outreach** | Identify High-Profile Events | Target events with 1000+ attendees, primarily in the Technology, B2B SaaS, and MarTech verticals. |
| | Initial Contact & Value Prop | Pitch the value of unlocking hidden ROI for their sponsors and increasing sponsor retention. |
| **Closing** | Partnership Structuring | Define the model (e.g., revenue share, package inclusion) and agree on terms. |
| | Contract Signed | Formalize the partnership agreement. |
| **Enablement** | Onboarding & Partnership Guide | Provide the organizer with the playbook on how to spot opportunities, handle objections, and sell BETA Labs to their sponsors. |

### 4.2. Sales Motion 2: Acquiring Sponsors (Beneficiary Customer)

This motion is focused on filling the "Event Catalogue" with paying sponsors.

**Refined Sponsor Sales Motion:**

| Stage | Step | Description |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Inbound/Outbound** | Source Lead | Can be an intro from an event partner, strategic outbound, or inbound from the website. |
| | Identify Intent Signal | **Timing Sweet Spot:** The prospect is most receptive ~1 month before the event. |
| **Follow-up** | Intro Call (30 min) | Qualify needs and introduce the "Premium ICP Connection" service. |
| | Post-Meeting Email | Send proposal, pricing, and relevant case studies. |
| **Selection** | Final Decision | Prospect confirms intent to move forward. Goal is to reach this stage within 5-7 days. |
| **Onboarding** | Contract & Invoice | Contract signed and 50% deposit paid. |
| | ICP Alignment Call (20 min) | Deep dive to finalize the Ideal Customer Profile for targeting. |
| | Deliverable | BETA Labs executes the matchmaking and delivers a list of confirmed meetings. |
| | Final Payment | Final payment is collected post-event or upon delivery. |

## 5. Pricing & Packaging Intelligence

The pricing model is flexible and depends on the sales channel.

- **Standard Model:** Performance-based pricing of **$100 per qualified meeting** paid by the sponsor.
- **Reseller Model (via Event Organizer):** BETA Labs is offered as an optional add-on to sponsors for a fixed price (e.g., **$1,500**), with the event organizer receiving a revenue share (e.g., **~15%**).
- **Bundled Model:** BETA Labs is included directly within an organizer's high-tier sponsorship packages (e.g., $10K-$15K+ packages) to increase the overall value and justify a higher price point.

## 6. Status & Roadmap

- **Sponsor Retention:** The first evidence of sponsor retention is emerging, with **CyberImpact** being re-engaged for a second event (Social Next Toronto), marking a key proof point for the model's ROI.
- **Internationalization:** Early-stage planning for international expansion is underway. While the current focus is **Canada/North America**, markets like **Dubai/Middle East** have been identified as potential future targets, though they would require a culturally adapted sales motion.
