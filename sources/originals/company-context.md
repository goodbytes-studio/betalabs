# Company Context: BETA Labs
**URL:** https://www.betalabs.ca/
**Research date:** 2026-03-11

---

## Overview
BETA Labs is a Montreal-based AI startup (Centech Propulsion, Summer 2025) that helps companies maximize their ROI at business events through intelligent pre-event meeting matchmaking. Their platform analyzes attendee data to identify high-fit contacts, then contacts, qualifies, and books confirmed 1:1 meetings between sponsors/exhibitors and their ideal customer profiles — before the event starts.

---

## Value Proposition

**For event organizers (primary customer):** BETA Labs is a revenue-share partner that enables organizers to offer a "Premium Qualified Leads" package to their top sponsors — creating a new revenue stream, differentiating their event offering, and increasing sponsor retention by delivering measurable ROI (confirmed meetings, not just badge scans).

**For sponsors/exhibitors (end beneficiary):** Replace unqualified conference conversations with pre-booked, confirmed meetings with high-intent ICPs. The promise: no more 3-day conferences spent chasing unqualified leads.

---

## Product / Service Offering

**Core offering: "Connexion ICP Premium" package**

The service is operated as a managed concierge (not a self-serve SaaS tool):
1. BETA Labs signs a revenue-sharing agreement with an event organizer
2. The organizer packages the service into a sponsor tier (e.g., Platinum add-on)
3. The sponsor defines their ICP with BETA Labs
4. BETA Labs receives the attendee list (under NDA) and uses their AI platform to identify ICP-matching attendees with high intent
5. BETA Labs contacts, qualifies, and books confirmed 1:1 meetings between the sponsor and their ICPs — pre-event

**Key product principle:** No technical integration required from the organizer. BETA Labs functions as a service layer, not a software integration.

**Confirmed deployments:**
- Startupfest — 70+ curated investor-startup matches
- ALL IN (Canada's largest AI conference) — "Pioneer Partner" finalist
- HOP! Le Sommet du commerce de détail 2025 (CQCD) — retail industry summit matchmaking

## Positioning

BETA Labs does not compete with event networking apps (Grip, Swapcard, Brella, EventMobi) — those are organizer-facing tools that BETA Labs deliberately positions around as an add-on layer. Their differentiation:

- **For organizers:** "We're not a competitor to your existing app. We're a premium service you offer on top of your existing packages to unlock a new revenue stream and guarantee sponsor ROI."
- **For sponsors:** "We don't sell visibility. We sell confirmed meetings. You only pay per qualified meeting held — it's pure performance."
- **Market positioning:** Event app incumbents are optimized for organizer metrics (engagement, check-ins). BETA Labs is optimized for sponsor pipeline metrics (qualified meetings, cost per meeting, meeting-to-opportunity rate).

## Core Use Cases

1. **Pre-event meeting booking for sponsors:** Sponsor defines ICP → BETA Labs identifies and contacts qualified attendees → confirmed meetings on calendar before the event starts
2. **Organizer upsell / new revenue stream:** Organizer packages BETA Labs service into Platinum tier or as a premium add-on at $100/qualified meeting (shared revenue)
3. **Investor-startup matching at startup conferences:** Curated 1:1 introductions between investors and relevant portfolio-fit founders (demonstrated at Startupfest)
4. **Retail/trade summit business development:** Connecting retailers, suppliers, and entrepreneurs at trade events (demonstrated at CQCD HOP! 2025)

## Buyer Personas

**Primary sale target (BETA Labs sells to):**
- **Event Director / Sponsorship Manager:** Responsible for sponsor satisfaction and renewal; feels the pain of sponsors who don't renew because they "didn't get enough from the event." Wants to offer something measurable and differentiated.
- **CEO/Founder of event company:** Revenue-driven; interested in adding new margin without development costs. Attracted by the revenue-share, zero-risk structure.

**End sponsor buyer (influences and validates):**
- **CMO / VP Marketing:** Economic buyer. Needs to justify event budgets; wants pipeline metrics not awareness metrics.
- **Field Marketing / Events Manager:** The daily pain carrier — books the travel, manages the booth, has nothing to show for it beyond badge scans.
- **RevOps / Sales Ops:** Technical gatekeeper — cares about CRM integration and data privacy compliance (Loi 25 in Quebec).

---

## Differentiators

- **Performance-based pricing:** Sponsors pay $100/confirmed qualified meeting — no meeting, no charge. Removes financial risk for the organizer's pitch to sponsors.
- **Organizer-agnostic execution:** Operates without requiring any technical integration — just an attendee list. Low friction to deploy.
- **Pre-event, not during-event:** Meetings are booked and confirmed before the event, unlike in-app matchmaking tools that rely on attendees self-selecting at the event.
- **ICP-defined qualification:** Meetings are filtered against the sponsor's exact ICP criteria, not algorithmic similarity scores.
- **Two-sided value creation:** Simultaneously creates new revenue for organizers AND pipeline for sponsors — a positive-sum model that builds loyalty on both sides.

## Business Model

- **Channel sales (indirect):** Revenue-sharing agreements with event organizers, not direct B2C/B2B sales to sponsors
- **Performance-based pricing:** $100 per qualified meeting confirmed (billed by the organizer to the sponsor)
- **Revenue share:** BETA Labs and the organizer split revenue (e.g., 50/50, negotiable by volume and exclusivity)
- **Stage:** Pre-revenue to very early revenue (Centech Propulsion, Summer 2025). No public funding disclosed.
- **Team size:** 2 confirmed (Elizabeth Tremblay, Founder + Nakita Rao, Co-founder). Likely <5 total.

## Strategic Notes for ICP Work

**1. Strategic tension between two internal documents:**
The "Rapport d'Intelligence" (external market analysis) frames BETA Labs as targeting sponsors/companies directly. The actual internal GTM Playbook (March 2026) describes a **channel sales model** where organizers are the primary customer. These two documents represent either (a) an active strategic pivot toward the channel model, or (b) two parallel motions being tested simultaneously. The web evidence (CQCD, Startupfest, ALL IN partnerships) supports the organizer-channel model as the operative motion. **This should be clarified early — the ICP for outbound prospecting depends entirely on which model is active.**

**2. No paying customers confirmed yet:**
No case studies, no press coverage, no public revenue signals. The event partnerships (Startupfest, ALL IN, HOP! 2025) may be pilot/in-kind arrangements rather than paid contracts. The company urgently needs 2–3 paying reference organizers to validate pricing and the revenue-share model.

**3. ICP for organizer-channel model:**
Best-fit organizers are Quebec B2B conferences with: significant sponsorship/exhibitor revenue, 200–2,000+ attendees, recurring format (annual), and sponsors who currently complain about poor ROI. Startupfest/ALL IN/CQCD events fit this profile. The ICP is NOT mega-conferences (too complex to move) and NOT small community events (too low revenue potential per sponsor).

**4. Critical unknown — product architecture:**
Does the matchmaking platform require organizer integration (attendee data system access), or does it work with a simple attendee list export? The GTM Playbook suggests the latter ("no technical integration required, just a list under NDA") — but this needs confirmation. The answer determines how easy it is to replicate the model at scale across new events.

**5. Loi 25 compliance:**
Quebec's data privacy law (Loi 25) is an active concern for any product handling attendee personal data. This is a real procurement gate for Quebec-based organizers and should be addressed proactively in sales conversations.
