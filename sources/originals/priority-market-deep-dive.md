## Vendelux Intelligence (Primary Competitor)

- **Revenue:** $2.1M in 2024 (up from $889K in 2023, +136% YoY)
- **Funding:** $16.4M total ($2.4M seed + $14M Series A, Nov 2023)
- **Team:** ~66 people
- **Database:** 250,000+ B2B events, proprietary attendee intelligence
- **Pricing:** Starts ~$20K+/year SaaS subscription. 50 meeting credits/year on Plus tier.
- **Model:** AI-generated outreach with human review. Automated calendar booking. Salesforce/HubSpot native.
- **Key metric:** 2.5x higher positive response rate, 6x higher meeting rate vs. industry averages
- **Target:** CMOs, demand gen teams at 500+ employee companies
- **Blind spot:** No organizer partnerships. No organizer distribution channel. Does NOT use actual attendee lists (uses predictive database).
- **Already indexing Toronto events:** Social Next Toronto 2026, DX3 Canada 2026 attendee lists published on Vendelux site.

### BETA Labs Advantages vs. Vendelux
1. Actual attendee data (organizer shares list under NDA) vs. Vendelux's predictive database
2. Pay-per-meeting pricing ($100/meeting) vs. $20K+/year SaaS commitment
3. Organizer distribution channel (revenue-share) vs. direct-to-sponsor only
4. Mid-market target (20-200 employees) vs. enterprise (500+ employees)

## Las Vegas: Key Data Points

### Sponsor Pain (Quantified)
- Average cost per lead at Vegas show: **$1,200** (vs. $400 at regional shows)
- 10x20 booth total investment: **$25,000-$55,000** (booth + show services + freight + travel add 40-60% to base cost)
- Only **37% of exhibitors** formally measure trade show ROI
- Badge scanning produces **70-90% unqualified contacts** (press, competitors, students, browsers)
- **42% of exhibitors increased** trade show budgets in 2025/2026

### Key Events
- CES: 142K attendees, 4,500 exhibitors. CES Foundry (new 2026) for in-person AI meetings.
- HIMSS: 25-28K attendees, 950+ exhibitors. Health IT.
- Channel Partners: 7,800 attendees, 350+ exhibitors. **Best first target.**
- ShopTalk: 10K attendees, 900+ exhibitors. Has own hosted buyer program ("Meetup").

### Competition Intensity: HIGH
- Vendelux already publishes CES event guides and attendee intelligence
- Swapcard powers multiple large Vegas events
- Grip growing presence

## Toronto: Key Data Points (NEW MARKET)

### Why Toronto Was Added
- Same country (no US registration needed), same timezone, 1hr flight
- Larger, more impersonal business culture vs. Quebec
- Elizabeth has warm thread (Cyberimpact at Social Next Toronto)
- **Collision Conference moved to Vancouver in 2025** — not a Toronto event anymore

### Key Events
- **PDAC:** 32,155 attendees, 1,300+ exhibitors, annual (Mar). World's premier mining convention. Record attendance in 2026.
- **Fabtech Canada:** 30K+, 1,500 exhibitors, annual (Jun). Same organizer as Detroit Fabtech.
- **Elevate Festival:** 10K+, annual (Sep). Sponsors: Google, TD, KPMG. Bizzabo is a sponsor.
- **CMTS:** 9K, 750 exhibitors, biennial (next: Sep 2027).
- **DX3:** 2K+, annual (Feb). Retail/marketing tech.
- **CMPX:** 15K+, 400 exhibitors, annual (Mar). HVAC/mechanical.
- **Social Next Toronto:** 700 attendees — too small (<1,000 floor not met).

### Competition Intensity: LOW-MEDIUM
- Vendelux indexing Toronto events but targets enterprise ($20K+/year), not mid-market
- Swapcard presence varies by event
- No direct competitor in the concierge matchmaking space
