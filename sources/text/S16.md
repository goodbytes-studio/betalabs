# 1. What Makes an Event an Easy Win?

Not all events are equal. BETA Labs' model works best at events with specific characteristics. This report scores events on 8 criteria to separate easy wins from hard sells.

| Criteria | Easy Win | Hard Sell |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Attendees | 5,000 - 30,000 (sweet spot) | < 1,000 or > 100,000 |
| Cross-industry dynamic | Tech vendors selling to non-tech buyers | Vendor-to-vendor (everyone is tech) |
| Existing matchmaking | None, or basic badge scanning only | Swapcard/Grip embedded, hosted buyer programs |
| Organizer accessibility | Independent association or mid-tier operator | Fortress (GSMA, CTA, Deutsche Messe) |
| Sponsor sophistication | No internal GTM tools, new market entrant | Has SDR team, uses Clay / Sales Nav already |
| Organizer pain awareness | Knows sponsors struggle, cares about retention | Never thinks about sponsor ROI |
| Frequency | Annual (recurring revenue) | Biennial or one-off |
| Travel from Montreal | < 2hr flight, same timezone | > 5hr flight, large timezone gap |

| Rule: if an event scores 6+ out of 8, it's an easy win. Below 4, stay away. |
| --- |

## 3.1 PDAC — Toronto

The World's Premier Mineral Exploration & Mining Convention

| Detail | Info |
| --- | --- |
| Date | March 7-10, 2027 |
| Venue | Metro Toronto Convention Centre, Toronto |
| Attendees | 32,155 (record in 2026, highest ever) |
| Exhibitors | 1,300+ across Trade Show, Investors Exchange, and Trade Show North (record in 2026) |
| Travel | 1hr flight or 5.5hr drive from Montreal. Same timezone (EST). |
| Organizer | PDAC (Prospectors & Developers Association of Canada) — independent association |
| Easy Win Score | 8 / 8 |

### Why It's Easy

Extreme cross-industry dynamic. Mining tech, environmental services, AI/data analytics, drilling equipment, and ESG consultants selling to mining executives, investors, and government officials from 130+ countries. Most exhibitors are NOT from the same world as the buyers.

New entrants everywhere. Mining is being flooded by tech companies (AI for exploration, ESG compliance, drone/remote sensing, battery minerals analytics). These newcomers have ZERO existing mining relationships. Perfect BETA Labs client.

No matchmaking competition. No Swapcard, no Grip, no hosted buyer program. Networking is old-school: walk the floor, attend receptions, hope for the best.

Organizer is an association, not a fortress. PDAC is accessible, member-focused, and would welcome a sponsor ROI service that improves exhibitor satisfaction.

Same country. No US registration, no cross-border tax, invoice in CAD. Eliminates every operational variable except product-market fit.

### Potential Friction

Veteran mining companies know everyone at PDAC. True for incumbents, but FALSE for the 200+ new tech exhibitors entering mining each year. Target the newcomers.

Mining culture is conservative. Buying a matchmaking service might feel unusual. Position as intelligence, not matchmaking.

# 4. Tier 2: Good Opportunities (After Tier 1)

Approach these after proving the model with Tier 1 events. They score 5-6 out of 8 and have minor friction points that make them better as second or third events in a market.

## 4.1 CMPX — Toronto

Canadian Mechanical & Plumbing Expo. 13,000+ attendees, 500+ exhibitors, Metro Toronto Convention Centre. HVAC, plumbing, and mechanical tech vendors selling to contractors, installers, engineers, and building managers. Strong cross-industry. No matchmaking competition.

Friction: Biennial (next edition: 2028). Cannot build recurring annual revenue.

Estimated net: ~$30,000. Difficulty: 4/10.

## 4.2 NAB Show — Las Vegas

National Association of Broadcasters. 54,000+ attendees, 1,100+ exhibitors, Las Vegas Convention Center. Media tech, broadcast equipment, streaming, AI for content. Tech vendors selling to broadcasters, production companies, media outlets.

Friction: Larger and more complex than Channel Partners (1,100 exhibitors is at the upper limit of current BETA Labs tech capacity). Better as Vegas event #2 after Channel Partners proves the model.

Estimated net: ~$95,000. Difficulty: 5/10.

## 4.3 CPMA — Toronto

Canadian Produce Marketing Association Convention. 5,000+ attendees, 700+ booths (record in 2026), Metro Toronto Convention Centre. Ag-tech, packaging, logistics, cold chain vendors selling to grocery retailers, distributors, and growers. Annual.

Friction: Produce/ag-tech is a niche BETA Labs hasn't validated. Sponsors may be smaller companies with lower ACV.

Estimated net: ~$18,000. Difficulty: 5/10.

## 4.4 Elevate Festival — Toronto

Canada's flagship tech & innovation conference. 10,000+ attendees, 200+ exhibitors. Corporate sponsors include Google, TD Bank, KPMG, Capital One. Senior decision-makers from tech, finance, health. Bizzabo is a sponsor (organizer is event-tech-aware). Annual, September.

Friction: Startup-heavy audience. Many attendees are founders seeking investors, not B2B buyers. Cross-industry dynamic is weaker than PDAC or Battery Show. 200 exhibitors is on the smaller side.

Estimated net: ~$18,000. Difficulty: 5/10.

# 6. The Informa Ladder Strategy

The single most important strategic insight in this report: one organizer relationship opens multiple markets.

| Informa Markets operates Battery Show (Detroit) AND Channel Partners (Vegas) AND 500+ other events globally |
| --- |

Why this matters: Instead of cold-pitching each event organizer separately, BETA Labs can build one relationship with Informa through The Battery Show (lowest-friction US event), prove the model, and then get warm introductions to Channel Partners, NAB Show, and other Informa events. The Battery Show team and Channel Partners team work under the same corporate umbrella.

### The Ladder

Step 1: Battery Show (Detroit, Oct 2027) — Prove the model with Informa. Low risk, low competition, same timezone.

Step 2: Channel Partners (Vegas, Mar 2028) — Warm intro from Battery Show team. First Vegas event at manageable scale.

Step 3: NAB Show (Vegas, Apr 2028) — Second Vegas event. Different industry, same city, same Informa network.

Step 4: Larger Informa events — HIMSS, Fabtech US, and other major Informa shows become accessible once 2-3 successful pilots are in the bag.

PDAC (Toronto, Mar 2027) runs in parallel on a separate track. PDAC is an independent association, not an Informa event. It serves as the validation pilot before the Informa ladder begins.
